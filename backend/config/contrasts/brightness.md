# Brightness

Each positive prompt asks for a bright, high, airy ambient sound; the negative prompt at the same position asks for the same kind of piece, dark, low and muffled. The pairs keep the genre ("ambient") and the absence of drums fixed. A direction made from them is a product of these prompts and of what the model that plays them does with them; each engine makes its own.

## Positive

- bright airy ambient pad, high shimmering tones, no drums
- sparkling glassy ambient texture, high register, no percussion
- light radiant ambient chords, crisp and clear, no drums
- shimmering bell-like ambient drone, high frequencies, no beat
- bright crystalline ambient soundscape, no drums
- airy luminous synth pad, treble-rich, no percussion

## Negative

- dark muffled ambient pad, low rumbling tones, no drums
- murky deep ambient texture, low register, no percussion
- heavy sombre ambient chords, dull and muffled, no drums
- deep subterranean ambient drone, low frequencies, no beat
- dark cavernous ambient soundscape, no drums
- thick murky synth pad, bass-heavy, no percussion

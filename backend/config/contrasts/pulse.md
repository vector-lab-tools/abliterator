# Pulse

Each positive prompt asks for an ambient piece with a steady rhythmic pulse or soft drums; the negative prompt at the same position asks for the same piece without any beat. The pairs differ in the presence of rhythm, the rest held as close as the wording allows.

## Positive

- ambient pad with a steady soft kick drum pulse
- warm ambient drone with gentle rhythmic percussion
- slow ambient synth chords over a steady beat
- dreamy ambient texture with soft hand drums
- ambient soundscape with a slow pulsing rhythm and hi-hats
- calm ambient music with a steady electronic beat

## Negative

- ambient pad, beatless, no drums
- warm ambient drone, no percussion at all
- slow ambient synth chords, free time, no beat
- dreamy ambient texture, no drums, no rhythm
- ambient soundscape, floating, beatless
- calm ambient music, no beat, no percussion

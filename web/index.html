On the word. Music models do not refuse, so there is no refusal direction here. "Abliteration" is used in an extended sense: steering and ablating concept directions in the model's residual stream while it plays. The directions are made from contrast prompts by difference of means and may be weak; published work finds many audio features readable but hard to steer.
